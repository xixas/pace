Setup step shown once per desktop, chosen by XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP. The extension lives in /usr/share/gnome-shell/extensions/pace@osmosys (shipped by the package); Enable runs gnome-extensions enable pace@osmosys. GNOME only picks it up after the next login. On KDE/COSMIC/XFCE/Cinnamon nothing is enabled: it only points at the tray icon.
